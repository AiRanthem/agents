# Main-agent delegation

These rules and the attached routing card apply to ordinary main-agent work. During explicitly started `advance-project`, use `codex/AUTONOMY.md` and fixed first-level roles; the controller manages those stages and each executor owns its assigned subtree. Keep main-agent responsibilities out of descendant assignments.

Retain ownership of the user's goal, consequential decisions, conflict resolution, integration and final synthesis. Honor user-designated roles and required independent reviews. Subagents stay within scope and may delegate further only when authorized.
