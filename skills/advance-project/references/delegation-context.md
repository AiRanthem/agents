# Prepare original context before delegation

Use this guidance for authorized multi-agent work, including ordinary skill execution. It does not start an autonomous project or change roles, permissions or independence gates.

Before dispatch, reuse current reads and inspect the smallest set of original material that the lead and descendants are likely to need: applicable instructions, the complete relevant contract, stable target/diff, and central interfaces, callers or test oracles. Bind evidence to source locations and the current artifact identity. Summaries are navigation; leave specialist-only exploration to its owner.

Give each executor its scope, authorization, required original inputs, evidence index and remaining gap. Share original evidence without importing author deliberations or prior review judgments into an independent review. An author's inherited history cannot establish independent review. Initial facet assignments follow a neutral review plan before the lead forms findings. Keep role responsibilities and permission boundaries explicit; controller routing does not make a child a controller.

Inputs already available and unchanged need no ceremonial reread. Re-read changed or missing inputs and honor mandatory instruction reloads; each reviewer still examines the critical code and test oracles and forms its own judgment. If independent context cannot be established, report the gap rather than claim review completion.
